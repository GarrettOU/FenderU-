## A. What drives reach for small product accounts, per platform (2026)

### TikTok (@fenderullc exists; manual posting)
- **Distribution model:** every video is tested on a small non-follower cohort first; the For You feed supplies ~7 of every 10 views (Metricool TikTok Study, 2,314,756 posts / 92,000 accounts, published 12 May 2026 — https://metricool.com/tiktok-study/). Follower count is not a direct ranking input; 44% of sub-100K accounts grew 2025→2026 (same study).
- **Length:** longer is not penalised. Buffer's analysis of ~1.1M TikToks (March 2025): videos >60s got **43.2% more reach and 63.8% more watch time** than shorter ones; 5–10s clips had a median reach of only 194 — https://buffer.com/resources/longer-tiktoks-get-more-views-data/. Practical target for a stills-plus-voiceover product video: **20–45s**, longer only if there is real content.
- **Hook window:** TikTok Shop's own creator content guide (reported 20 May 2026) prescribes a **3–6 second hook** then key message then CTA — https://ppc.land/tiktok-shops-shoppable-video-content-guide-tells-creators-what-actually-works/ (secondhand report of the TikTok doc).
- **Video vs. photo carousel:** conflicting. Metricool 2026: video gets 5x views / 6x interactions vs images. Fanpage Karma (≈700K posts, Jan–May 2025): carousels +3% reach, **+81% engagement, +82% likes, −33% shares** vs video — https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/carousel-vs-video-performance-tiktok-instagram/. Read: carousels are a cheap, legitimate second format for a stills-only operation; test both.
- **Cadence:** no primary TikTok guidance in 2026. Vendor consensus is 3–5/week; Socialinsider's 2026 TikTok benchmarks report posting frequency up 40% while audience growth slowed 33% (teaser figures only — https://www.socialinsider.io/fr/rapports/tiktok-engagement-benchmarks). Consistency over volume.
- **Search-first:** Adobe's 2026 survey (n=1,007): **49% of US consumers have used TikTok as a search engine** (41% in 2024), Gen Z 65%, Gen X 40%, boomers 12% — but only 4% of Gen Z prefer it over Google — https://www.adobe.com/express/learn/blog/using-tiktok-as-a-search-engine. Put the literal search phrase ("boat fender covers", "8.5 x 26 fender cover") in on-screen text, spoken audio and caption; hashtags are secondary.
- **Music for business accounts:** a TikTok Business account is limited to the **Commercial Music Library** (pre-cleared), not trending mainstream audio. Documented since 2020 **[older]** (https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/tiktok-changes-rules-on-music-usage-by-businesses/577734/) and unchanged in 2026 guides (https://www.soundstripe.com/blogs/why-can-i-only-use-commercial-sounds-on-tiktok). Own voiceover + CML track is the safe default.
- **Shop mechanics:** US TikTok Shop seller needs a US business entity/EIN, ID and US bank; approval reported at 2–6 business days (https://printify.com/blog/tiktok-shop-registration/). Follower minimums for *sellers* are disputed between guides; the 1,000-follower rule is well documented for *affiliate creators*. Without TikTok Shop, the only outbound link is the bio link (bio links generally need 1,000 followers on personal accounts; Business accounts get a website field). Standing rules note organic posting via API needs an audited app — manual posting stands.
- **What gets suppressed / banned (political):**
  - **Paid:** TikTok's ads policy (last updated July 2026) prohibits paid political advertising and explicitly bars ads for *"merchandise that features prohibited individuals, prohibited entities, or prohibited content, including campaign slogans, symbols, or logos"* — https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/tiktok-ads-policy-politics-government-and-elections?lang=en. **"Make America Great Again" is a registered campaign slogan, so FenderU cannot run TikTok ads or use Promote.** Same page: organic political content is allowed within Community Guidelines.
  - **Organic:** Community Guidelines (released 25 Aug 2026, effective 24 Sep 2026) restrict Government/Politician/Party accounts (no ads, no e-commerce/gifting — first announced Sept 2022 **[older]**, https://newsroom.tiktok.com/en-eu/political-accounts-policy-updates) but say nothing about ordinary merchants selling political-themed goods — https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/accounts-features. No evidence of down-ranking of flag/MAGA product content per se.
  - **TikTok Shop listings:** the US Prohibited Products Policy (updated May 2026, as summarised by https://www.echotik.live/blog/prohibited-products-list-on-tiktok-shop-2026/, 2 Jun 2026) bans "products promoting hate, discrimination, violence, hateful ideologies or illegal activity." Printify's TikTok Shop US guidance (https://help.printify.com/hc/en-us/articles/25281619814545) adds products glorifying "violent political organizations" and warns *"if you're unsure, we advise against using content related to these attributes or topics, as it may result in shop suspension."* A flag/MAGA fender cover is not on the prohibited list, but it is a judgment call at listing review. **Honest read: TikTok Shop is possible but carries suspension risk; TikTok ads are off the table.**

### Instagram Reels (account exists; not API-linked, so manual)
- **Ranking:** Mosseri's stated top signals (Jan 2025) are watch time, likes per reach, **sends per reach** (shares via DM) — widely reported, primary post not retrieved. Reels go to a small non-follower test group first.
- **Length:** cap raised to **3 minutes (Jan 2025)**; multiple secondary sources report Reels over 3 min are not recommended to non-followers. For stills-based product Reels, 15–30s is realistic; total watch time and replays beat raw length (secondary, https://www.dataslayer.ai/blog/instagram-algorithm-2025-complete-guide-for-marketers).
- **Trial Reels** (launched Dec 2024): post to non-followers only, off-grid, insights after ~24h; Meta claims 40% of users post more and 80% of those saw more non-follower reach — https://9to5mac.com/2024/12/10/instagrams-new-trial-reels-feature-lets-creators-test-content-with-non-followers (company-reported). Useful for a zero-follower account because there is nothing to lose.
- **Cadence:** Instagram's one published figure is that top-growth creators post 10+ Reels/month; Buffer (Aug 2025, secondhand) found the biggest growth jump moving from 1–2 to 3–5 posts/week — https://instantdm.com/blog/how-often-to-post-on-instagram.
- **Watermarks:** Instagram has said since Feb 2021 **[older]** that Reels "visibly recycled from other apps" (logos/watermarks) and blurry/low-res video are made less discoverable; reiterated Jan 2025 (https://jennstrends.com/?p=10590). Export clean files, never a TikTok-watermarked download.
- **Search:** Instagram's own statement (via Hootsuite): the search text is matched against usernames, bios, **captions**, hashtags and places — https://blog.hootsuite.com/social-media-updates/instagram/instagram-search-seo/. Mosseri has said hashtags do little for reach. Lead captions with the plain product phrase.
- **Grid:** profile grid moved to **3:4 crop (Jan 2025)**; a 9:16 Reel cover loses top and bottom; keep the title in the middle ~55–60% of the cover (https://www.kapwing.com/resources/instagrams-new-grid-layout-size-and-dimensions-2025/).
- **Music:** Instagram's licensed library is for personal, non-commercial use; some business accounts and post types lose it. Meta's **Sound Collection** is cleared for commercial use including ads — summarised at https://usethirdchair.com/blog/instagram-sound-library-rules-for-creators-and-brands. Same answer as TikTok: own VO + cleared track.
- **Shopping:** as of **September 2025 Shops on Facebook and Instagram use website checkout**; product tags still work on feed, carousel and Reels but send buyers to your site — https://www.shopify.com/blog/how-to-sell-on-instagram. Requires Commerce Manager + a catalog + a domain. For FenderU that is doable (one product) and makes Reels tappable to fenderu.com.
- **Political-content limit (this is the key Meta fact):** Meta's 9 Feb 2024 post defines political content as content *"potentially related to things like laws, elections, or social topics"* and says it will not proactively recommend it on **Explore, Reels, In-Feed Recommendations and Suggested Users**; followers still see it in Feed and Stories; professional accounts can check **Account Status** to see if recent political posts cost them recommendation eligibility — https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/continuing-our-approach-to-political-content-on-instagram-and-threads (updates dated 7 Jan 2025 and 28 May 2025). On **8 Jan 2025 Mosseri reversed the opt-in default**: political recommendations are now on at **"standard"** by default with "less"/"more" options — https://www.engadget.com/social-media/threads-and-instagram-will-recommend-political-content-after-all-232343107.html. **Implication:** a MAGA-print product Reel can be classed as political and can still be shown to non-followers, but users who chose "less" will not see it in Reels/Explore, and the classifier is opaque. Check Account Status after the first few posts; the owner-approved framing ("Patriotic · America · America First · MAGA") on the product is the content, so there is no way to make it non-political — plan around it, don't fight it.

### Facebook Reels / Page (Page automated: daily photo + weekly reel; Groups and Marketplace manual only)
- **Reach:** organic Page reach is consistently reported at 1–6% of followers; Reels is the format that reaches non-followers (vendor consensus, e.g. https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/the-facebook-algorithm-2026-explained-what-marketers-need-to-know/). Groups reportedly reach 10–40% of members **[vendor claim, no Meta data]**.
- **Political setting on Facebook:** the "political content" control sits under **Content Preferences** (Meta update 25 Apr 2025) and the default is "Default" (i.e. no additional reduction); users can choose to see less. Applies to Feed, Reels and Watch — https://about.fb.com/news/2021/02/reducing-political-content-in-news-feed/ (2021 **[older]**, with 2025 updates). Same practical posture as Instagram.
- **Ads/boosts:** any boosted post Meta classifies as about "social issues, elections or politics" requires **advertiser authorization and a "paid for by" disclaimer**, is kept in the Ad Library for seven years, and AI edits must be disclosed — Meta, Feb 2026 (updated 1 Jun 2026 and 27 Oct 2026) https://about.fb.com/news/2026/02/meta-prepares-for-2026-us-midterms/. A MAGA product boost will almost certainly be flagged; the restriction window (27 Oct–3 Nov 2026) has passed, but the authorization requirement has not. Do not boost without going through authorization.
- **Links:** external links in posts reportedly cut reach 30–50% **[vendor claim]**; keep fenderu.com in the first comment or Page CTA rather than the post body.
- **Reels mechanics:** standing rules already record that silent video is rejected (add a silent AAC track) and `UploadVideo` works where `uploadAReel` 422s.

### Pinterest (automated, five boards)
- **Formats:** Idea Pins were folded into standard Pins on **11 May 2023 [older]**; all Pins now take links, scheduling and product tags; video Pins max **5 minutes**; ideal aspect 2:3 (1000×1500) for static, 9:16 accepted for full-bleed video — https://community.pinterest.biz/t/pin-creation-announcement-and-faq/1847.md.
- **Length:** Pinterest's promoted-video guidance is 6–15s; organic product videos of 15–30s with a text title in the first frame are the norm (vendor, https://www.b2w.tv/blog/pinterest-video-strategy).
- **Shop mechanics:** Product Pins come from a connected catalog feed (Shopify/WooCommerce or manual feed) refreshed at least every 24h; requires claimed website and merchant-guideline acceptance — https://www.auditsocials.com/blog/pinterest-product-tagging-catalog-ads-compliance-guide-2026 **[vendor, unverified]**. Without a catalog, standard Pins still carry a direct link, which is Pinterest's main advantage over TikTok/IG.
- **Political:** Pinterest has refused political campaign ads since 2018 and expanded EU restrictions in Oct 2025 (https://opentermsarchive.org/en/memos/pinterest-expands-definition-of-prohibited-political-advertisement/). No published rule against organic political merchandise; evidence on how Pinterest ranks it is **thin**. Pinterest is search-driven, so titles like "American flag boat fender covers" matter more than any algorithm quirk.

### YouTube Shorts (no account mentioned; lowest priority)
- **Model:** seed-audience test then expand; "Viewed vs. Swiped Away" is the key metric; since 31 Mar 2025 any play counts as a view. vidIQ (updated 9 Oct 2026): Shorts of **13s or 60s** perform best in a 35-billion-view dataset, first **1–3 seconds** decide the swipe, channels with **200+ Shorts** see consistent growth — https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-algorithm/. Shorts max length rose to 3 minutes in Oct 2024 **[older, from prior knowledge]**.
- **Shop:** YouTube Shopping affiliate threshold reportedly dropped to **500 subscribers (March 2026)** but still requires YPP membership (https://www.pulse.bot/entertainment/news/youtube-cuts-affiliate-shopping-threshold-eligibility-to-500-subscribers-as-platform-race-heats-up-7c4dbf85-bbc4-4650-b74b-63212e0cbb35/ — secondary). Not reachable from zero this season.
- **Political:** no evidence of organic suppression of political merchandise; Google Ads political rules apply only to paid.

### Cross-platform: AI labels
- Meta requires users to disclose realistic AI-altered video or audio and may penalise failure (Feb 2026 midterms post above). TikTok requires an AIGC label for realistic AI content and may auto-apply it (https://www.echotik.live/blog/tiktok-ai-content-labeling-rules-2026/). **Relevance:** FenderU uses no AI imagery (good). Brag Rags' only product imagery is **Runway AI mockups** (decision 2 Nov 2026) — toggle the AI-generated label on when posting them; an un-labelled AI mockup is a policy risk. Whether a synthetic voice alone triggers the TikTok label is **unconfirmed**; a human voiceover avoids the question.

## B. Recurring formats that work for physical products — and which are doable from stills + text + voiceover

| Format | Why it works | Minimum production | Doable with stills + screen text + VO only? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **ASMR unboxing / "packing orders"** | Sound-led, high retention, builds trust in a real small business; one shop's packing video drew 1.6M views (single case) — https://sproutsocial.com/insights/?p=149742 | Phone, tripod, the actual box, tape, tissue; no talking required | **No** — needs real footage. (Owner won't shoot; skip unless he changes his mind. A tabletop packing clip is the single highest-value 2 minutes of footage he could ever shoot.) |
| **"Watch me install it" / demo** | Shows the product doing the thing; TikTok Shop guide: show the product in the first 3–6s | Phone + the product on a fender | **Partial** — a 3-still sequence (product-on-white → close detail → on boat) with "slips on in seconds, no tools" text approximates it. Weaker than real motion but legitimate. |
| **Before / after** | Fast visual payoff; satisfies in <5s | Two matching shots | **FenderU: no** — there is no approved "bare fender on the same boat" shot and compositing is banned. **Brag Rags: yes** — "stale lodge merch" (text card, no competitor imagery) → Runway mockup. |
| **POV** ("POV: you pull up to the dock…") | Puts the viewer in the scene; works with a single strong image and a text line | One hero still + text | **Yes** — Malibu or Tigé still, slow Ken-Burns push, one text line. |
| **"Things you didn't know existed"** | Novelty + search intent; carries a product with no explanation needed | Hero still + VO | **Yes** — FenderU is genuinely a product most boaters have not seen. |
| **Founder talks to camera** | Trust, "who answers the email"; Facebook favours "native, unpolished" | Phone selfie clip | **No as video; yes as VO** — owner voice over stills carries most of the trust effect. Wording must be approved copy only. |
| **Text-on-screen storytelling** (listicle: "fits these fenders", "3 things to know") | Sound-off friendly, search-indexable, repurposes to carousel | Stills + captions | **Yes** — the core format for both businesses. |
| **UGC-style review** | Social proof; TikTok Shop's highest-converting format | A customer's phone clip | **Not yet** — FenderU's approved "$20 back for a real photo, first 20 customers" offer is the only legitimate route to this material. Never fake it. |

Stitch boat-care or dock-rash clips instead. |

## C. Where each product will most plausibly sell

### FenderU — ranked
1. **Facebook (Page + manual Groups + Marketplace).** Audience fit is strongest: 25–35 ft boat owners skew 45+, and Facebook boat-owner / wake-boat / lake groups are where they already trade gear. Groups reportedly out-reach Pages 5–10x **[vendor claim]**. Marketplace gives a shipped-goods listing with a photo and price that Google also indexes; fee is **10% of order total on shipped sales** (majority of 2026 reseller guides, e.g. https://blog.vendoo.co/everything-you-need-to-know-about-facebook-marketplace-fees — conflicting minority says 5%; verify in Commerce Manager); Pages list via a Commerce Manager shop, personal profiles via the normal flow (https://closo.co/blogs/platform-specific-guides/can-a-business-post-on-facebook-marketplace). Standing rules: Groups and Marketplace are his hands only; Group posts are plain photos, no overlay type. Political-content setting is "Default" (not reduced) for most users. Boosting needs political-ad authorization.
2. **Pinterest.** Already automated, direct links on every Pin, search-driven (people search "boat fender covers"), no political-ad exposure because nothing is paid. Low volume but durable; Pins keep surfacing for months.
3. **Instagram Reels (manual).** Good visual fit, Trial Reels de-risk a zero-follower start, product tags can point to fenderu.com once Commerce Manager is set up. Downside: "less political" users will not be reached; no API, so each post is manual.
4. **TikTok (manual).** Reach engine is the best in class and follower count is irrelevant, but: the buyer demo is under-represented, ads/Promote are prohibited for MAGA merchandise, and TikTok Shop listing carries suspension risk. Worth posting (zero marginal cost once the video exists) — not worth building the business on.
5. **Etsy.** Etsy's Prohibited Items policy (updated 11 Aug 2026; last updated 6 Oct 2026) bans hate items, violent items and content "that promote, support or glorify hatred," and lists protected classes including immigration status — https://www.etsy.com/legal/prohibited. Patriotic/flag/MAGA merchandise is not prohibited and is widely sold there, but analysts note Etsy removed "political affiliations" from its welcome list in March 2024 **[older]**, which they read as higher removal risk for divisive items (https://www.valueaddedresource.net/etsy-prohibited-items-policy-update-political-activism-violence-hate/). Fees: $0.20 listing, **6.5% transaction** on price+shipping, **3% + $0.25** processing, Offsite Ads 15% (12% above $10K/yr) if a sale comes through them — ≈$9.95 on $100 before Offsite Ads (https://checkoutpage.com/blog/etsy-fees). On $119 that is roughly $11.70–$29 per sale. Standing rules: Etsy OAuth consent is his click. Fine as a listing, not a growth channel.
6. **YouTube Shorts.** Nothing stops it, but no account, no shop eligibility, and the boat-owner audience is better reached on Facebook. Post the same file if there is zero extra effort; expect little.

### Brag Rags (B2B lodges; hunter stores are B2B2C) — ranked
1. **Direct email + a phone call**, in that order. It is the only channel with a 3-week horizon to the Nov 16–Dec 1 store window. Benchmarks: Belkins measured **0.45%** average cold reply across 7.5M emails in 2025 (https://belkins.io/blog/cold-email-response-rates.md); Smartlead calls 2–4% good and <1.5% broken; Apollo calls 3–6% good for 2026 (https://www.apollo.io/insights/what-is-a-good-benchmark-for-reply-rates-in-cold-outreach). **Two replies from 40 (5%, one a stop) is at or above benchmark; the sample is just tiny.** Warm intros from outfitters Garrett has hunted with (open thread #3) beat any of this.
2. **Facebook (Page as proof-of-legitimacy; outfitter/guide Groups manually).** Lodge owners check the Facebook Page before replying to a cold email — a Page with a few real-looking videos is a conversion aid for email, not a lead source. Mallard Bay's case studies show outfitters themselves "hustling on Facebook groups" for bookings (https://mallardbay.com/for-guides/blog/from-stressed-to-fully-booked-how-black-oak-outfitters-turned-243-leads-into-growth-with-mallard-bay — vendor). Groups of outfitters/guides exist but no member counts could be verified; most have no-promotion rules — read the rules first. **Evidence for B2B lead flow from Groups: thin.**
3. **Instagram.** Where lodges post their own trip photos and where hunters follow lodges; the right place for the hunter-facing "brag" assets a lodge can repost. Not a lead channel this season.
4. **LinkedIn.** Personal profiles get 2.8–8x the reach of Company Pages (vendor range, https://dsmn8.com/blog/the-social-media-managers-guide-to-the-linkedin-algorithm-in-2026/), and 9:16 native video is reported as the top organic format. But lodge owners and guides are lightly represented on LinkedIn; corporate-gift buyers (companies that book lodges for client hunts) are the only LinkedIn-native segment. Low priority until a case study exists.
5. **TikTok / YouTube Shorts.** Hunting content does well, but the buyer is a lodge owner, the product is a quote, and there is no shop to link. Use only to repurpose whatever gets made for Facebook/Instagram.
- **Seasonality:** `revenue-plan.md` already shows the money is in December (Arkansas: 51 of 60 hunting days fall Dec 10 or later; Christmas gifts land in the same weeks). A follower-building effort started 9 Nov cannot compound in time; social this season is about looking real, not about reach.

## D. Building a following from zero in 2026 — realistic benchmarks

- **No credible study gives "posts until traction."** The best available: vidIQ says channels with **200+ Shorts** see consistent growth; Socialinsider finds a TikTok video peaks ~2 weeks after posting and most growth happens in the first 5 days (https://socialinsider.io/blog/tiktok-virality-insights); Metricool 2026 found **>25% of TikTok accounts with 2K–10K followers moved up a tier vs ~10% on Instagram** (https://metricool.com/press-release-2026-social-media-study/). Translation: plan on **8–12 weeks of 3–5 posts/week (~30–50 posts)** before judging a platform, and expect TikTok to move first.
- **Consistency vs volume:** Buffer (Aug 2025) found the growth jump at 3–5 posts/week; Socialinsider shows posting up 40% while growth fell 33% — more volume is not buying more growth in 2026. Pick a cadence that can run for 12 weeks without new footage: for FenderU that is 2–3 posts/week per platform from a stills library.
- **Comments/replies:** every platform's public guidance treats replies as engagement signals; TikTok "reply with video" and IG comment replies also generate new posts for free. On a MAGA product expect hostile comments; a stated rule (reply to buyers and fit questions, hide/ignore politics) protects the account and the owner's time. Instagram's Account Status should be checked after the first batch to see whether anything was made ineligible for recommendation.
- **Cross-posting:** watermarked (TikTok-logo) files are explicitly demoted on Instagram (since 2021, reiterated 2025) and listed as non-original under TikTok's FYF eligibility (secondary); YouTube's penalty is widely reported but **not confirmed** by YouTube (https://timetopost.co/blog/tiktok-to-youtube-shorts-without-watermark/). Always export the clean master from the editor and upload natively everywhere.
- **Identical vs tailored:** post the **same master video** everywhere, but tailor (a) the caption — plain search phrase first on IG/TikTok/Pinterest, no link in the body on Facebook; (b) the cover frame — mid-frame title for IG's 3:4 grid, 2:3 static for Pinterest; (c) audio — CML/Sound Collection track or own VO only; (d) AI label on anything containing a Runway mockup. Facebook favours less polish; Pinterest favours a readable title in frame 1.

## E. Tooling for platform-grade vertical video from stills + VO + text

- **What small brands use:** **CapCut** (desktop/mobile; auto-captions, keyframe zoom on stills, TikTok-native export) and **Canva** (templates, brand kit, simplest UI, exports 9:16 MP4). Common workflow: build cards/overlays in Canva → assemble and caption in CapCut (https://www.swellai.com/blog/capcut-vs-canva). Pricing is unstable across sources: Canva Pro ≈ $15/mo; CapCut Pro ≈ $10–24/mo depending on tier and store (https://flowith.io/blog/capcut-pricing-2026-free-vs-pro-vs-team). **CapCut's June 2025 Terms update** granted broad rights over uploaded media and restricts commercial use on the free plan — a paid plan or Canva avoids that (https://fluxnote.io/guides/capcut-questions-answered). Alternatives already in this stack: Adobe Express and the existing `mk_reels2.py` / `reelmaker.py` generators (ffmpeg) — the Python path is the only one that can be run without Garrett touching a dashboard.
- **Voiceover:** record on a phone (his voice is the trust asset) or an ElevenLabs/vidIQ-style TTS; if synthetic, label as AI on TikTok to be safe. Every spoken line must be approved copy.
- **Safe zones (1080×1920 master):** keep all text and the product inside a **centred ~900×1400 box**. Working margins: **top ~15% (≈290 px), sides 10–15% (≈110–160 px), bottom 25–35% (≈480–670 px)** — reap.video, 7 Sep 2026, https://reap.video/blog/short-form-video-safe-zones. Meta's Reels *ad* spec cited by third parties is 14% top / 35% bottom / 6% sides (≈269 / 672 / 65 px). TikTok's right-hand button stack starts ~840 px down; keep text left-of-centre. Preview with "Save to drafts" in each app before publishing.
- **Caption placement:** burn captions in (most Shorts/Reels viewing is sound-off), lower-middle, never in the bottom quarter, 2 lines max. Platform auto-captions may also appear — keep yours high enough not to collide.
- **First-frame rules:** product visible and legible in frame 1; one title line in the middle 55–60% (survives IG's 3:4 grid and Pinterest's crop); no logo-only openers; for FenderU the first frame is always a cover **on a fender, on a boat** (standing rule).
- **Spec:** 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264, AAC audio (Facebook rejects silent video — add a silent AAC track), ≤60s unless there is real content, clean export with no watermark.
