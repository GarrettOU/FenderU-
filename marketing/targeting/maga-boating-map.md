## The pattern that matters

Trump boat parades are an election-year event. 2020 and 2024 were huge
(Lake Travis 2,000+ boats, Table Rock ~1,500, Jupiter 1,538, Lake Keowee
~800, Indian Lake ~600). 2025 was thin: the only parades found were June 14
(Flag Day, Trump's birthday) in Jupiter and Fort Myers. 2026 produced one
findable listing (Jupiter, June 14). Nothing is announced for 2027. The one
date that has held every year since 2020 is **June 14**. The next real peak
is 2028. So: be set up as a sponsor/vendor before June 14, 2027, and be the
first name the lake groups see when they wake up for 2028.

## Parade calendar
Memorial Day weekend (Havasu, Jupiter) > **June 14** (the durable one) >
July 4 weekend > Labor Day weekend (the 2020 mega-weekend) > Sept/Oct in
election years (LOTO Sept 22 2024; Jupiter Oct 13 and Orange Beach Oct 19
2024). Expect 2027 to look like 2025 and 2028 to look like 2024.

## What each platform allows a small seller

- **Facebook groups:** no platform rule against selling; each admin sets it
  (typical: no promos, or one promo post a week in a pinned thread). Parade
  groups are run by GOP clubs or volunteers; the proven way in for a brand is
  sponsoring the event, not cold-posting product.
- **Facebook Marketplace:** physical goods are listable; political merch is
  not on the prohibited list. Precedent: Meta banned the apparel page
  American AF (1.6M followers) over "Trump" branding it called misleading.
  Use "American flag fender cover" wording in the listing title and avoid
  campaign logos [Meta's own policy page could not be read: unconfirmed].
- **Meta boosted posts / ads:** ads "about a political figure, political
  party..." fall under Meta's Social Issues, Elections or Politics policy and
  need advertiser ID verification and a "Paid for by" disclaimer, or they are
  disapproved. A boosted post showing the MAGA print will likely be classed
  that way; a flag-only creative runs as a normal product ad. Political
  affiliation targeting was removed in Jan 2022, so "boating interests + ZIP
  radius around the lakes + lookalikes of Page engagers" is the targeting.
- **Rumble Ads (self-serve):** $100 minimum wallet, $30 minimum daily budget,
  real-time-bid CPM, exact-match keyword targeting on video titles ("boat",
  "parade", "lake"). Third-party benchmark ~$6 CPM video [unconfirmed, 2024].
- **Telegram paid posts:** direct deals with channel owners or via telega.io;
  benchmark $100-500 per post at 5-20k subscribers, $500-3,000 at 20-100k,
  $3,000+ above 100k [unconfirmed until quoted]. Official Telegram Ads only
  via resellers from about EUR 500 and text-only.
