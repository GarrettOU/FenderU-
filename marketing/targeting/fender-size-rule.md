# Who uses the 8.5 x 26 fender (targeting rule for the reps)

Researched 27 Oct 2026. Every claim has a source; items no page confirmed are
marked [unconfirmed].

## The one-line answer

The 8.5 x 26 (and 8.5 x 27, Polyform G-5 8.8 x 26.8) is the fender for
**25 to 35 ft boats**. The next size down (6.5 x 22/23, Polyform G-4) is for
**20 to 25 ft**. The next size up (10.5 x 30, Polyform G-6, Big B 10 x 26) is
for **35 to 50 ft**. FenderU's cover fits the fender that 25 to 35 ft boats
carry, and that is the whole targeting logic.

## Which boats actually carry it

- **25-35 ft cruisers and sport boats: the core market.** All charts agree. A
  Ranger Tugs R-27 ships from the factory with Polyform G-5 (8.8 x 26.8);
  owners of 23-25 ft walkarounds and a heavy 24 ft Chris-Craft report 8"
  fenders. Freeboard and dock conditions move individual owners up or down
  (a 27 ft Carver owner on 6", a 34 ft owner on 12").
- **Sea Ray's own branded fender kit is Big B 6 x 15 for 20-25 ft.** The
  small Sea Rays (SPX/SDX under 25 ft) are a 6" market.
- **Pontoons and tritoons: 8.5" starts at about 25 ft.** The best-selling
  pontoon length is 22 ft (Bennington 22 LSB, Barletta C22UC, Bentley 223,
  Crest 220), and 22-24 ft pontoons and rental fleets run 6.5". Owners of
  25 ft+ tritoons report 8.5 x 27 and 8 x 26; one 25 ft owner ran 10 x 26
  and called it overkill. Bennington's site groups "over 26 ft" separately.
  The 25 ft threshold is an inference from the charts [unconfirmed].
- **Wake boats: confirmed NOT this size.** WakeMAKERS' pick for wake boats is
  a 6" x 15" center-hole fender; the wake-market Mission Sentry is a flat
  22 x 9 x 5.5 pad; wake boats run 20-26.5 ft (MasterCraft X22-X26, Malibu
  22-26 LSV). Only the biggest 25-26 ft Nautique G / Malibu 26 owners move
  up, and to a Big B 8 x 20, not 8.5 x 26.
- **Houseboats and 35 ft+ yachts: above this size.** Houseboat Magazine
  recommends 10 x 26 up to 50 ft; charts put 35-50 ft at 10.5-12". A
  different product, unless FenderU adds a bigger cover.
- **Sportfish and center consoles 25-35 ft: yes by chart** (Tuff End 8 x 27 =
  25-40 ft), but no owner reports were found [unconfirmed].

## Tells a rep can read on a marina's or dealer's website

1. **Slip lengths.** Marinas cap boats at about slip length + 10%. Mostly
   30-40 ft slips = 25-35 ft boats = 8.5". Slips topping out at 25 ft = 6.5".
   40-55 ft slips drift into the 10.5" market.
2. **Model numbers encode length.** Sea Ray 260-350, Formula, Regal,
   Chaparral, Cobalt, Crownline, Monterey, Rinker, Chris-Craft in the 26-35
   range = 8.5". Carver and Cruisers Yachts 35 ft+ = 10"+. (Model-by-model
   lineups for Cobalt, Formula, Regal, Chaparral and Crownline were not
   verified in this pass.)
3. **Pontoon pages.** 25 ft+ tritoon lines (Bennington 25-27 Q/R, Barletta L
   and Lusso 25+, Harris Crowne/Grand Mariner 25-27, Premier 25+, Avalon
   25-27) = yes. A dealer whose featured pontoons are 20-23 ft = 6.5" account.
4. **Words.** "Cruiser", "express", "sport yacht" = 25-35 ft = yes. "Yacht",
   "houseboat", "sedan bridge" = 35 ft+ = bigger fender.
5. **Bad signs.** Rental fleets of 20-24 ft pontoons; wake-boat-only dealers
   (Malibu, MasterCraft, Nautique, Supra/Moomba, Tige, Centurion); bass and
   fishing-boat dealers; PWC-heavy marinas.
6. **When the site is ambiguous, read the dock photos.** Taylor Made and West
   Marine both tell boaters to look at what the neighbouring boats hang; a rep
   can count fenders on the 26-32 ft boats in a marina's own photos.

## Is 8.5 x 26 the most common size?

Not on inland lakes. No public sales-by-size data exists [unconfirmed either
way]. Amazon and Walmart best-seller badges sit on 5.5-6.5" multi-packs as
often as on 8.5 x 27. Because the volume of inland-lake boats is 18-24 ft
(22 ft pontoons, 20-24 ft bowriders, 20-26 ft wake boats), the 5.5-6.5" class
almost certainly outsells 8.5" on a typical lake. The 8.5" is the volume size
only where 25-35 ft cruisers and 25 ft+ tritoons concentrate, which is why
Lake of the Ozarks' cruiser marinas and the Duck Creek arm of Grand Lake are
the right water, and a marina of under-26 ft boats (Holden Beach) was not.

## The rule for the reps, in one paragraph

Sell where the boats are 25 to 35 feet. Qualify a marina or dealer by what its
website says about length: slips mostly 30-40 ft, inventory of express
cruisers and sport boats with model numbers in the 26-35 range, 25-27 ft
tritoon lines, and the words "cruiser", "express", "sport yacht". Skip
marinas whose slips top out at 25 ft, fleets of 20-24 ft rental pontoons, and
wake-boat-only dealers; treat "yacht", "houseboat" and 40-55 ft slips as a
bigger-fender market. When in doubt, look at the dock photos and count the
fenders on the 26-32 ft boats.
