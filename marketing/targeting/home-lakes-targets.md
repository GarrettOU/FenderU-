# Home-lake targets: Lake of the Ozarks (MO) and Grand Lake O' the Cherokees (OK)

Researched 27 Oct 2026. Fit test: boats of roughly 25-35 ft and up use the
8.5 x 26 fender FenderU's cover fits; under-25 ft boats, most wake boats and
rental pontoon fleets do not (see `fender-size-rule.md`). Emails are only
those published on each company's own domain; nobody has been contacted from
this list. Several dealer sites block automated reading and are marked
"unverified": confirm by phone before a rep writes.

## Lake of the Ozarks

**Boat mix.** The region's big-cruiser lake. MarineMax Lake Ozark's 189 wet
slips are all for 32-58 ft boats; Toad Cove Yacht Club's covered slips run
30-72 ft; Cruisers Yachts picked the lake's MarineMax stores to sell its
34-50 ft line in 2024. The big-boat marinas: MarineMax Lake Ozark, Toad Cove
(Camden on the Lake), Millstone (7MM), Village Marina (125 covered slips),
Big Thunder's Glencove Marina and Ozark Yacht Club, Formula's marina.

## Grand Lake O' the Cherokees

**Boat mix.** Mixed, with a real big-boat segment on the Duck Creek arm and
around Monkey Island (yacht and cruiser water); the northern end (Grove,
Bernice) is pontoon country. Cherokee Yacht Club's 170+ covered slips run
30-70 ft, Thunder Bay's 120+ covered slips run 34-70 ft, Honey Creek
Landing's 100 covered slips run 30-60 ft, Harbors View goes to 60 ft.
Arrowhead Boat Sales (Cobalt, Regal, Barletta; four ship stores, five
marinas including Cherokee and Arrowhead Yacht Clubs) is the dominant dealer
and the single most efficient buyer on the lake.

## Season
Late October: every dealer is promoting winterization, Hammerhead is closed,
gas docks are on fall hours. Parts counters and sales offices stay open all
winter, and that is when a store decides what to stock for spring.
