## 4. How a post goes out (phone, about five taps)

TikTok: open the app as @fenderullc, "+", upload the draft from the phone, "Add
sound", search the song by name, set it to play from the start, keep the video
at full length, paste the caption, post. Instagram: "+", Reel, pick the draft,
"Add audio", search the song, paste the caption, share. The song is attached by
the platform under its licence; the file itself stays silent.

Account type matters: TikTok **Business** accounts and Instagram **professional
business** accounts are limited to the royalty-free commercial library and
cannot pick these songs. Set TikTok up as a personal account and Instagram as a
Creator account (or stay personal). Both still get a link in bio
(FenderU.com), which is where every buyer is sent.

What the connector can do instead: once Garrett connects Instagram to vidIQ, a
scheduled task can publish the reels for him, but **without a song** (the API
cannot attach licensed music). That is a fallback, not the plan he asked for.
